Hi friends, this is Yang.
An undergrad studying Mathematics and Music Composition, while juggling multiple research assistant jobs (and enjoying it very much!).

Projects that I'm currently working on:
Cognitive Map Reorganization in Sequential Memory Task with James Whittington and Jo Warren

Characteristics of Efficient-Coding Neural Network under Energy Efficiency Constraint with John Vastola in Kanaka Rajan lab

Modeling Episodic Simulation through Memory Recombination with Marcelo Mattar

===

My goal is to understand imagination/mental simulation.

I'm particularly fascinated by the work that starts from a specific theoretical commitment: a normative constraint, an objective, or an architecture, and derives from it a neural algorithm that the brain might be using. On this view, the behaviors such algorithms produce are what we call cognitive abilities.

Memory retrieval is another major interest of mine, for one cannot imagine without retrieving one's memories and potentially recombining them.
