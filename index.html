What I'm after

My goal is to understand imagination (an interchangeable term might be mental simulation).

It's a broadly-defined term, so I think there are some specific proxy topics to start from:

  • Navigation — cognitive map as a platform of imagination, specifically by doing replay or rollouts on it. A cognitive map can also be about something abstract and non-spatial!

  • Visual imagery — essentially "close your eyes and think about an apple". There are some interesting properties such as non-commitment found in this.

  • Intuitive physics — "look at this tower of blocks, would it fall?"

  • Theory of mind — "what do you think others would think of you if you do this?"

A one-liner that concludes what I want to do:

circuit models that explain the why or how of imagination as a cognitive ability

(e.g. why is there non-commitment in visual imagery, neurally?)

Not sure if wanting to connect theoretical neuro and computational cogsci is too ambitious, but that's what I'm building my skillset towards.
